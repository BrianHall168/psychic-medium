The Difference Between a Psychic and a Medium: A Complete Guide

D By Daniel M. · March 2026

The terms psychic and medium are used interchangeably so often that most people assume they mean the same thing. They do not. While the two gifts are related and sometimes coexist in the same person, they are fundamentally different abilities that serve different purposes.

Understanding the distinction helps you choose the right type of reading for your needs – and sets appropriate expectations for what you will experience.

What Is a Psychic?

A psychic is a person who perceives information beyond the five physical senses. They read energy – the energy of people, situations, relationships, and potential outcomes. This ability is sometimes called extrasensory perception or intuitive reading.

Psychic abilities manifest in several forms. Clairvoyance is the ability to see images, symbols, or visions related to a person’s situation. Clairsentience is the ability to feel emotions and physical sensations connected to others. Claircognisance is the ability to know things without logical explanation. Clairaudience is the ability to hear words, names, or messages intuitively.

A psychic reading focuses on the living. Your reader tunes into your energy field and the energy surrounding your question – whether it is about a relationship, a career decision, a family situation, or your personal growth. They may perceive past events that are influencing you, present dynamics you are not consciously aware of, and potential future outcomes based on current energy trajectories.

The key word is living. A psychic reads the energy of the living world. They do not necessarily communicate with spirits.

What Is a Medium?

A medium is a person who communicates with spirits – the consciousness of people who have physically died. The word medium literally refers to their role as an intermediary between the physical and spiritual worlds.

During a mediumship session, the medium raises their energetic vibration while the spirit lowers theirs, creating a meeting point where information can be exchanged. The medium then relays what they receive – descriptions, personality traits, memories, names, and messages – to the living person seeking connection.

Evidential mediumship specifically focuses on providing verifiable evidence that the communication is genuine. A skilled evidential medium will share details about the departed person that they could not have known through normal means – physical appearance, cause of death, specific memories, characteristic phrases, or personality traits that the living person recognises.

The key word is departed. A medium connects with those who have passed from the physical world. Their primary function is not reading your energy but facilitating communication between you and the spirit world.

Can Someone Be Both?

Yes, and this is where the term psychic medium comes from. A psychic medium possesses both gifts – the ability to read the energy of living people and situations, and the ability to communicate with the departed.

Not all psychics are mediums, and technically not all mediums are psychics, though most develop psychic sensitivity through their work. But those who possess both gifts at a high level can offer a uniquely comprehensive experience – blending spirit communication with practical life guidance in a single session.

This combination is rare and highly valued. It means that during your session, the medium can connect with your departed loved one, share evidence and messages, and then transition into reading your personal energy to provide insight about your current life challenges.

How to Choose

Choosing between a psychic and a medium depends entirely on what you need.

Choose a psychic if you want guidance about a living relationship, a career decision, your life path, or a personal challenge. A psychic reads the energy of your current situation and provides insight to help you navigate it.

Choose a medium if you want to connect with a departed loved one, receive evidence of their continued existence, or hear messages from the other side. A medium facilitates spirit communication.

Choose a psychic medium if you want both – and many people do. A combined session allows you to honour your spiritual connections while also addressing the practical realities of your life.

Common Misconceptions

One common misconception is that mediums can summon any spirit on demand. In reality, spirits come through by their own choice. A skilled medium sets the intention and opens the channel, but they cannot force a specific spirit to communicate. Most of the time, the intended spirit does come through – but occasionally someone unexpected arrives first.

Another misconception is that psychic readings and medium sessions should feel frightening or intense. In practice, both are typically experienced as calm, warm, and comforting. Psychic readings often feel like a conversation with someone who understands you deeply. Mediumship sessions, while sometimes emotional, are overwhelmingly described as peaceful and healing.

A third misconception is that you need special preparation or belief for either type of session to work. You do not need to believe – you simply need to be open. Genuine psychic and mediumistic ability does not depend on the client’s faith. The evidence speaks for itself.

The Value of the Combined Gift

In our experience, the most transformative sessions are those that combine both gifts. A client might come seeking connection with their departed mother and, during the session, receive not only her mother’s messages but also psychic insight about a career decision she has been struggling with – insight that turns out to have been influenced by her mother’s guidance from the other side.

This seamless blend of the spiritual and the practical is what makes psychic mediumship so powerful. It addresses the whole person – their grief, their questions, their path, and their connection to something larger than the physical world.

If you are considering a session and are not sure which type you need, a psychic medium session covers all bases. Your medium will follow the energy wherever it leads – and the result is almost always more than you expected.
