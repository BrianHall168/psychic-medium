What is the difference between a psychic and a medium?

A psychic reading generally focuses on a living person’s situation: relationships, work, choices, or personal direction. A medium reading focuses on a claimed spiritual connection with someone who has died. Some practitioners offer both, but the focus of your question is a useful way to choose where to start.

A reading is a personal, spiritual experience. It cannot guarantee a specific outcome or replace practical information, professional care, or conversations with people involved. You can ask a reader about their approach before booking and stop if the session does not feel right.
